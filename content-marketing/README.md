## Content Workflow

1. Research the project, product or campaign.
2. Identify the audience and communication objective.
3. Extract the most important value proposition.
4. Convert technical information into accessible messaging.
5. Adapt the message to the platform and audience.
6. Publish, monitor engagement and respond to community feedback.
7. Refine future content based on audience response.

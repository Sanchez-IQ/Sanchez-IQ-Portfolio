# Web3 Content — Technical Storytelling & Community Education

## Overview

**Focus:** Web3 content creation, technical storytelling, educational communication and community engagement

## The Problem

Web3 products often involve technical concepts that are difficult for new or non-technical community members to understand. Effective community content needs to preserve the important idea while making it accessible, useful and engaging.

## My Approach

I use a research-to-publishing workflow:

1. **Research** — understand the product, update, narrative or campaign.
2. **Extract the value** — identify what matters to the target audience.
3. **Simplify** — translate technical concepts into clear language without overstating claims.
4. **Structure** — build a hook, logical flow and clear takeaway.
5. **Adapt** — tailor the message for the platform and audience.
6. **Engage** — use questions, calls to action, AMAs or community discussion where appropriate.
7. **Refine** — observe feedback and improve future communication.

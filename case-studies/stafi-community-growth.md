# StaFi Protocol — Community Growth & Operations

## Overview

**Role:** Community Lead / Discord Admin  
**Organization:** StaFi Protocol  
**Focus:** Community growth, moderation, education, engagement and crisis communication

## Challenge

The role required sustaining an active global Web3 community across multiple channels while improving participation, supporting users and maintaining clear communication during major protocol events.

## Role

I managed and nurtured the StaFi community across **Discord, Telegram and Twitter**, combining community operations with content, education and user-facing communication.

## Approach

- Built consistent community engagement across multiple platforms.
- Used educational campaigns to make complex staking concepts easier to understand.
- Coordinated AMA sessions and incentive programs.
- Maintained clear communication during high-traffic product events.
- Used moderation and support systems to improve community quality and response times.

## Execution

### Community Growth

Scaled community membership from approximately **7,000 to more than 15,000 users**, representing more than **150% growth within a year** according to my CV.

### Engagement

Orchestrated AMAs, educational campaigns and incentive programs associated with a reported **40%+ increase in active participation and user retention**.

### Content & Education

Produced **threads, videos and graphics** designed to simplify complex staking concepts for community members.

### Community Operations

Implemented moderation bots and ticketing systems, with a reported **60% reduction in spam reports** and improved technical-support response times.

### Crisis Communication

Led community-facing crisis management during high-traffic events such as the **StaFi 2.0 launch**, helping maintain clear communication and mitigate misinformation.

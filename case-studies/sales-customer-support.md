# Sales & Customer Support — Real Estate

## Overview

**Role:** Sales Representative / Customer Support  
**Organization:** Success Haven Real Estates  
**Period:** 2021–2024  
**Focus:** Lead generation, customer relationships, property sales and CRM-supported customer service

## Challenge

The role combined sales performance with customer support: understanding client needs, matching customers with suitable properties, maintaining relationships and resolving issues efficiently.

## Approach

- Identified customer pain points and suitable property options.
- Built relationships through regular contact and follow-ups.
- Used storytelling, VIP onboarding and discounts as part of customer-retention efforts.
- Helped investors make informed property decisions.
- Documented, tracked and monitored customer issues through CRM processes.
- Maintained product knowledge to support customer needs and sales targets.

## Documented Results

### Lead & Sales Generation

Generated **over ₦100M in leads/sales** from local real-estate properties across states, according to the supplied CV.

### Customer Retention

Achieved a documented **40% repeat-customer rate** through VIP onboarding, discounts and storytelling-based sales pitches.

### Customer Support

Provided product/service information, alternatives to customer preferences, order processing and timely resolution of customer queries.

## Skills Demonstrated

Sales · Customer Support · Relationship Management · Negotiation · Follow-Up · CRM · Lead Generation · Customer Retention · Problem Solving · Communication

## Evidence

The role, dates, responsibilities and performance figures are based on the Sales / Customer Service CV supplied for this portfolio.

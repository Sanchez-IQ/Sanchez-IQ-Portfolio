# Amasiri Research Project

## Sedimentary Characteristics and Reservoir Potential of the Amasiri Area

### Project Type

Academic geological research project

### Discipline

Sedimentary Geology · Reservoir Characterization · Petroleum Geoscience

## Project Overview

This research project investigates the **sedimentary characteristics and reservoir potential of the Amasiri area**. It represents an application of geological observation, sedimentary interpretation and reservoir-quality thinking to an academic research problem.

The project is included in this portfolio as evidence of my geological research, technical writing and interpretation capabilities.

## Research Focus

The study is centered on understanding the sedimentary characteristics of the investigated area and assessing what those characteristics may indicate about reservoir potential.

Key geological considerations include:

- Sedimentary characteristics and facies-related observations
- Lithological and textural characteristics
- Depositional interpretation
- Reservoir-quality considerations
- The relationship between sedimentary properties and potential fluid-storage characteristics
- Geological evidence supporting reservoir-potential assessment

## Why This Project Matters

The project demonstrates how a geological investigation can move from description of sedimentary materials toward interpretation of their potential significance for subsurface applications. It also provides a foundation for developing practical skills relevant to energy, mining, environmental, water and geotechnical geoscience.

# StaFi Protocol — Community & Ecosystem Contribution

## Overview

StaFi Protocol is one of the key ecosystems represented in my Web3 portfolio. My contribution has focused on community support, moderation, content creation, ecosystem education, ambassador activities, and promotional communications around liquid staking and related DeFi infrastructure.

## Role & Contribution Areas

- Web3 community management and moderation
- Community support and user-facing communication
- Educational and promotional content creation
- Ambassador and ecosystem engagement
- X/Twitter content and thread development
- Telegram community support
- AMA preparation and question curation
- Community calls and Space participation
- Product and ecosystem updates
- Governance and proposal communications

## Selected Topics Covered

### Liquid Staking & rTokens

Created educational and promotional content around StaFi's liquid staking ecosystem, including rTokens and their role in providing liquid representations of staked assets.

### rToken App & StaFiHub

Supported communications around the merged rToken App and its multi-ecosystem staking experience, including updates covering supported networks, tokens, staking products and ecosystem functionality.

### LSaaS — Liquid Staking as a Service

Created and curated content around StaFi's LSaaS direction, including the LSaaS App, LST deployment guidance, modular infrastructure, point modules and integrations involving LST/LRT ecosystems.

### StaFi 2.0

Communicated progress and ecosystem developments associated with StaFi 2.0, including rToken migrations, LRT stack development and the expansion of liquid staking infrastructure.

### Governance & DAO Communications

Supported educational communications around StaFi governance and community proposals, helping community members understand product, infrastructure and governance developments.

## Community & Content Workflow

My work typically involved:

1. Understanding a technical product or ecosystem update.
2. Researching the key user-facing value and implications.
3. Translating technical information into clear community language.
4. Preparing posts, threads, announcements or discussion prompts.
5. Supporting community questions and follow-up discussions.
6. Monitoring engagement and maintaining constructive conversations.
